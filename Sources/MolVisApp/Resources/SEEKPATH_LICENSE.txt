Attribution for incorporated SeekPath 2.1.0 data and logic
===========================================================

mcrysden incorporates band-structure k-path definitions and selection logic
from SeekPath 2.1.0 (https://github.com/giovannipizzi/seekpath), which is
released under the MIT license below.

The k-path data (k-parameters, special-point coordinates, edge lists) in
Sources/MolVisApp/HPKOT.swift is transcribed from
seekpath/hpkot/band_path_data/ of SeekPath 2.1.0.

The aP variant-selection algorithm (Niggli reduction, M2/M3 axis/sign
transforms, aP2/aP3 selection) in Sources/MolVisApp/HPKOT.swift follows the
logic in seekpath/hpkot/__init__.py of SeekPath 2.1.0.

Reference: Y. Hinuma, G. Pizzi, Y. Kumagai, F. Oba, I. Tanaka,
"Band structure diagram paths based on crystallography",
Computational Materials Science 128 (2017) 140-148.
DOI: 10.1016/j.commatsci.2016.10.015
